Who we are

A locally owned Nigerian engineering company.

OSA-IDE Nigeria Limited is incorporated and registered in Nigeria, specialising in building and civil engineering construction, general project management and supervision.

Our directors and site management teams bring experience across engineering, building construction, environmental services and project management. Our clients include government institutions, corporate organisations, property developers and private individuals.
